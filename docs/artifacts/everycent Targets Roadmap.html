8a

Target model and monthly targets

Done

One target per category, stored and edited through the API, with the simplest cadence first. The engine answers one question for any category and month: how much more does the target ask for? Every later stage reuses that answer.

Includes

  • Schema: category_target, one row per category (cadence, behavior, amount + currency, due day, start month); repeat and snooze columns come with 8b/8d
  • API: PUT/DELETE /categories/{id}/target; categories carry their target; month responses add target: {needed, funded, underfunded} per category
  • Monthly targets with “Set aside another…” and “Refill up to…” behaviors
  • Engine: targetNeed(history, target, month, currentMonth) — needed, funded, underfunded — table-tested against the YNAB cases
  • Inspector Target section and editor (I need with math, By, Next month I want to; Assign button); row note “$60.00 more needed by the 15th” / “Funded”; yellow Available with a pie while underfunded, a check when met
  • Fresh start copies targets, starting in the fresh start's month

Depends on

  • Phase 6 month history; Phase 7 payment categories (targets there come in 8e)

Confirmed (help center)

  • “Set aside another…” asks for the full amount every period, even if last month's money rolled over; money builds up
  • “Refill up to…” asks only to replace what left the category, up to the target
  • Refunds don't count toward this period's target; they count from the next period
  • Looking at a future month, a “Refill up to…” target asks for the full amount: leftover money only counts once the month begins
  • Editing a target changes it in every month, past and future; one target per category at a time
  • Underfunded is yellow with a pie icon; met shows a green check; zero and not underfunded is grey

Found in YNAB (API + app, 3 Oct)

  • Set aside counts only this month's Assigned: carried money, spending and refunds don't
  • Refill counts carried-in + Assigned; same-month spending and refunds don't, moving money out does
  • In a future month Refill ignores carried money (the API still counts it; the app doesn't)
  • A target applies from its start month, which is the month it was created from; saving from an earlier month moves the start back
  • The due day only changes the wording

Still open

  • Percentage rounding; a due day past the month's end; a met target at $0.00 Available; $0 targets; undo for target edits verify.md
8b

Weekly, yearly and custom targets

Done

The remaining cadences differ only in how a period is defined and how its amount is spread over months. Keep the period math in the engine so 8a's need calculation stays one function.

Includes

  • Weekly: amount per week on a chosen weekday; the month asks for as many weeks as that weekday occurs
  • Yearly: amount by a “By” month, spread over the months left in the period; repeats annually
  • Custom: amount by a “Due on” date; repeat off (one-time), monthly or yearly every N; “Set aside…”, “Refill up to…” and “Have a balance of…”
  • Target editor form for every cadence and behavior; inspector copy for each

Depends on

  • 8a target model and need calculation

Confirmed (help center)

  • Weekly totals depend on how many times the chosen day falls in the month (5 occurrences ask for 5 weeks)
  • Yearly and repeating custom targets track money assigned across the whole period, not what's in the category now
  • A new period starts the month after the due month, even if the due date is earlier in that month
  • “Have a balance of…” isn't meant to be spent from before the due date and can't repeat
  • A one-time custom target stops asking after its due month

Found in YNAB (app, 3 Oct)

  • Dated targets spread what's still missing over the months left, rounding up: $1,000 by Dec 30 asked $333.34, then $333.33, then the rest
  • Created with 3 months left, a yearly target asks a third, not a twelfth
  • Set aside counts the period's Assigned; refill counts what the category holds; a new period starts the month after the due month
  • “Have a balance of” is the old Target Balance (by Date): without a date it never asks this month; the form has no Monthly Savings Builder
  • “On Track” when this month's share is in: green pill with a part-filled pie

Still open

  • Spending during the month vs a dated refill/balance; Assigned before the start month; the custom “Fill up to” heading; weekly refill numbers verify.md

  • Views bar has Underfunded, Overfunded and Snoozed next to the built ones (seen in YNAB, 6d)

Found in YNAB (API + app, 7 Oct)

  • Underfunded per category = meet the target or cover overspending, whichever is more (O11 asked $100 not $130; O13, met, asked $30); overspent categories without a target ask their overspending
  • Card payment categories ask what's owed beyond Available, less this month's credit overspending; cards with credit overspending are listed apart (“may need more attention”) but still funded
  • With nothing selected (or a view) it funds overspending, then dated-this-month by date, end-of-month monthly, later due dates, card payments, and stops at $0 Ready to Assign; a selection is funded in full
  • Reduce Overfunding takes back this month's Assigned above what the month asks, capped by Available (O1 −$30 of $50); refill counts carried money, so Assigned can go negative (O4 −$50); dated targets compare against this month's share (O10 −$66.66); payment categories give back Available above what's owed
  • Views list what each option would change; empty states “Cheers! You don't currently have any underfunded categories.” / “You don't currently have any overfunded targets.”
  • “Cost to Be Me / October's Targets” = each target's month amount (dated: this month's share), current month only, plus “Next month's targets could increase your total to …”

8e

Credit card payment targets

Done

Payment categories get their own two target types. They reuse 8a's model but measure progress differently: a fixed monthly payment, or the card's balance paid off by a date.

Includes

  • “Pay Specific Amount Monthly”: asks for a fixed amount each month, whatever was paid or spent
  • “Pay Off Balance by Date”: monthly amount = what's owed beyond Available, spread to the date; re-computed every month
  • Payment-category inspector: target type, progress against the whole balance, “Create a Debt Payoff Target”
  • Payment targets in Underfunded; never snoozable

Depends on

  • 8a–8c; Phase 7 payment math (7a–7d)

Confirmed (help center)

  • Two types only for payment categories; specific-amount progress is for this month only
  • Pay-off-by-date progress is Available against the whole balance; uncovered credit overspending raises next month's ask
  • Funded card spending moving into the payment category doesn't change what the target asks

Found in YNAB (API + app, 8 Oct)

  • Pay off by date spreads what's owed beyond Available over the months left, both ends counted, rounding up: $120 by March asked $20.00, $100 by December $33.34
  • This month's Funded Spending is left out of both sides (E2: $110 owed, $10 funded → “Pay off $100.00”, 0%); credit overspending raises the ask at once ($125 → $20.84); a payment without money set aside changes nothing
  • Specific amount asks like a monthly “Set aside another…”; Underfunded asks the target, not the balance; Reduce Overfunding gives back Assigned above the ask (E3 $20.00, E1 $29.16); card targets count in October's Targets
  • The Target section replaces the Payment alert, except while the category is red; no row note, no snooze; one payment category's Auto-Assign reads “Paid Last Month”, “Average Paid”
  • A pay-off month in the past leaves no target

  • Positive card balances wait for 7e; “Making minimum payments?” link and “Cover Overspent Categories” not built deferred.md

Ground rules

  • Match YNAB one to one; check, don't assume. The YNAB API reports what targets ask for (goal_under_funded, goal_overall_left, goal_months_to_budget) per month, so most gates can be settled with a fresh category per case. Wait before reading after a write and confirm deltas both ways (CLAUDE.md).
  • Targets never move money. They add fields to month responses; Available, Activity and Ready to Assign stay exactly as Phases 6–7 compute them.
  • Money stays exact. Target amounts are bigint minor units with a currency; spreading an amount over months or weeks uses the engine's allocation and rounding, never floats.
  • The server owns target math. The client renders needed, underfunded and progress from the API; the engine function is pure and table-tested.
  • Build one vertical stage at a time. Engine rule → PostgreSQL integration test → API schema → generated client → UI → browser parity check. Run pnpm check before marking a stage done.
